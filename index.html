FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is AWSM for?

Freelancers, remote workers, small teams, and students preparing for exams. Anyone who needs a quiet, reliable place to work or study.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome when desks are free. To be sure of a seat, message us on WhatsApp before you come.

Which passes include a locker?

Lockers are included with monthly passes only, with a refundable key deposit of ₹300.

How do access cards work?

Weekly, 10-day and monthly members get an access card for entry. Please return it when your pass ends. If a card is lost, a replacement fee is charged for the new card.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes. A special discount is available for students with a valid student ID. Message us on WhatsApp for the current student rates.

Can I book the meeting room separately?

Yes. The reading and meeting room can be booked by the hour. Message us for rates and availability.

Is there a washroom I can use?

Yes. Separate washrooms for men and women are available on the same floor, just a few steps from our office.

What's the difference between the two monthly plans?

Both run for 30 days. The 9-hours-a-day plan is ₹3,000 (usually ₹4,000) and the 12-hours-a-day plan is ₹3,500 (usually ₹5,000) during our limited-time launch offer.
